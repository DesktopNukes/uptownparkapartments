Becoming a Resident

Review the basic qualifying procedures before applying for a studio apartment at Uptown Park Apartments.

Qualifying Procedures

Every person applying for residency at Uptown Park Apartments must complete a rental application. The application must be completed by the actual resident, and a separate application is required for any person contributing income to the household.

A $50.00 non-refundable application fee is required per application.

Income

Base income requirement is $1182 per month.

All listed income sources must be verifiable and will be verified, including salary, hourly wages, commissions, bonuses, social security, retirement, and investment income. Copies of supporting documents, such as tax returns or 1099 forms, may be required.

Credit & Criminal Process

A credit and criminal history will be obtained for each applicant. If an applicant has no credit history, it is accepted as qualifying credit.

Credit history is reviewed for slow payment, delinquent accounts, collections, liens, judgments, and public records. Applicants with poor credit of 7-10 negative credit lines will be charged an additional $50.00 deposit.

Applicants with more than 10 negative credit lines will be required to have a co-signer or prepay the lease term. Applicants convicted of a felony against a person or property, any drug-related felony, or five charges of any type other than minor traffic citations will be declined. Registered sex offenders are ineligible.

Residency

If prior residency was in a rental property, verification must be made with the owner or agent of that community.

Acceptance may be considered when no derogatory information is obtained from the previous landlord concerning timely payment, property damage, or compliance with lease terms.

Occupancy Standards

Studio apartments measure 240 square feet and can accommodate one occupant.

Lease Term

Lease terms are a minimum of six months and a maximum of one year. Terms are at the discretion of Uptown Park Apartments, based on the market or the owner/agent's instructions.

Deposits

The standard deposit for a studio apartment home is $350.00.

The deposit is a non-refundable holding fee. If you cancel your move, the deposit will be forfeited.

The owner or agent may grant exceptions to acceptance standards or application qualifications if exceptions are justified. Special circumstances may determine the final decision after all standards and qualifications are reviewed.

Felony & Criminal Charge Disclosure

Applicants warrant that neither they nor any household member have criminal charges pending, awaiting disposition, looming in any way, in the process of being filed, or awaiting filing by any government entity.

Applicants also warrant that neither they nor any household member has been involved in activity that may result in arrest or criminal charges. If tenancy is accepted and a criminal charge is later learned of by the landlord, the landlord may immediately terminate tenancy by eviction or otherwise.
